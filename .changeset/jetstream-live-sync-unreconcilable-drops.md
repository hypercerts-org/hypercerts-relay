---
'hypercerts-relay': patch
---

Live ingestion no longer stops on an upstream `#sync` event it cannot reconcile. A resync whose replacement records do not match the `#sync` DID or revision, that repeats a record key, that carries a record key wider than the archive column, or whose envelope cannot be stored previously ended the live consumer with a fatal error, and the restart looped on the same event until an operator intervened, stopping archival for every account. These are now treated as upstream input the archive cannot represent: over-wide rows are dropped (counted under `field_too_long`), and an event that cannot be reconciled is dropped whole before anything is published (counted under `invalid_rev` or `field_too_long`). An empty resync is applied only when the verifier's recorded revision for the account matches the revision the `#sync` named; otherwise it is dropped rather than treated as an empty repository. The upstream cursor advances past a dropped event and, where repository recovery is configured, the account is offered to it on a best-effort basis. Storage, archive, and attribution failures remain fatal. No operator action is required.
