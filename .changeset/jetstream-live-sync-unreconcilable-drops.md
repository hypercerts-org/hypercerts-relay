'hypercerts-relay': patch
---

Live ingestion no longer stops on an upstream `#sync` event it cannot reconcile. A resync whose replacement records do not match the `#sync` DID or revision, that repeats a record key, that carries a record key wider than the archive column, or whose envelope cannot be stored previously ended the live consumer with a fatal error, and the restart looped on the same event until an operator intervened, stopping archival for every account. These are now treated as upstream input the archive cannot represent: over-wide rows are dropped (counted under `field_too_long`), and an event that cannot be reconciled is dropped whole before anything is published (counted under `invalid_rev` or `field_too_long`). An empty resync is only applied when the revision of the repository that was fetched is the revision the `#sync` named. The upstream cursor advances past a dropped event, the affected repository is queued for recovery, and its next commit is verified against the archived state. Storage, archive, and attribution failures remain fatal. No operator action is required.
